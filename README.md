## What this repo is

A thin case-study shell for:

- naming the research role in plain language
- describing **public-safe** process work (methods habits, collaboration patterns, tools in the open)
- keeping a firewall between portfolio copy and anything participant- or clinic-adjacent

It is **not** a research data repository, **not** a paper preprint, and **not** a place for field exports or identifying detail.

Independent notes on the site are Jade's own write-ups ... not IU-endorsed research and not peer-reviewed.
