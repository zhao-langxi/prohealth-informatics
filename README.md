## What this repo is

A thin, public case-study shell for:

- naming the research role in plain language
- describing **public-safe** process work (methods habits, collaboration patterns, tools in the open)
- keeping a firewall between portfolio copy and anything IRB-, participant-, or clinic-adjacent

It is **not** a research data repository and **not** a paper preprint.

Independent notes on this site are Jade's own write-ups ... not IU-endorsed research and not peer-reviewed.

---

## What was actually done (public-safe)

High-level process work only ... no field content, no participant detail, no outcomes claimed:

- Structured analysis and documentation tasks on a research platform serving vulnerable populations (platform name and study specifics stay private)
- Reproducibility habits: versioned notes, explicit scope boundaries, and checklists before anything touches public copy
- Collaboration with the research group on analysis workflows that can be described without raw data or identifying detail

Do **not** read metrics, publications, clinical outcomes, or product launch claims into this scaffold. They are omitted until real and cleared for public wording.

## Throughline (short)

Same instinct as the rest of the public work: drawn to the unglamorous parts of systems that have to work for real people ... the handoff after the demo, the clause nobody reads until it matters, the form that assumes you already belong. Research process here is about precision and accountability at the documentation layer, not about impressing with numbers.
